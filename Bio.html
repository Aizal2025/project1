Biography

At the age of 40, he has established himself through consistent effort, discipline, and responsibility. He currently lives in Chhatarpur, South Delhi, while his early life was spent in Kidwai Nagar. His background has played a key role in shaping his practical thinking and strong work ethic. His leadership reflects coordination, management skills, and commitment toward his work.

Overall, his journey showcases perseverance, responsibility, and continuous growth in both personal and professional aspects.
